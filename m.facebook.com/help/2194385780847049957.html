What do I do if someone is harassing me in an app connected to Facebook?
Although Meta doesn’t control the support provided by app developers, we work to make sure that all outside developers follow our policies. Please report the problem to the app developer directly. If you don’t get a satisfactory response, please report the app to us.
We recommend that you block the person who's harassing you. This will prevent them from seeing your information or contacting you in any way on Facebook. They won't be notified that you've blocked them.
