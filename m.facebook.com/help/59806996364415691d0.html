Manage your Close Friends list on Facebook
You can add or remove friends from your Close Friends list on Facebook.
  1. Go to your friend's profile.
  2. On your friend's profile, scroll down and tap See Friend Lists.
  3. Tap to select Close friends.
  4. Tap Done.
Note: You can add friends to more than one list or remove friends from a list.
