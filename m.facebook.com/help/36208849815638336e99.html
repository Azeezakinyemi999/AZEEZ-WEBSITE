Manage your Meta Verified creator subscription
This article is about Meta Verified for creators on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the Meta Verified for businesses Help Center.
Meta Verified creators that have successfully completed the verification process can manage and make changes to their subscription.
Learn about managing your Meta Verified creator subscription below.

Viewing subscription details

You can view subscription details including verification status, verified profile(s), and benefits.

View your subscription

Facebook mobile app (iPhone)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Facebook:
  1. Open the Facebook mobile app and log into your verified account.
  2. Tap in the bottom right, then tap .
  3. Tap Go to Accounts Center, then tap Meta Verified.
Facebook mobile app (Android)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Facebook:
  1. Open the Facebook mobile app and log into your verified account.
  2. Tap in the top right, then tap .
  3. Tap Go to Accounts Center, then tap Meta Verified.
Facebook website (Desktop)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Facebook:
  1. Log into your verified Facebook account on a computer.
  2. Click your profile picture in the top right.
  3. Click Settings & privacy, then click Settings.
  4. Click Accounts Center in the left menu, then click Meta Verified.
Instagram mobile app (iPhone)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Instagram:
  1. Open the Instagram mobile app and log into your verified account.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  3. Tap in the top right, then tap Meta Verified.
Instagram mobile app (Android)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Instagram:
  1. Open the Instagram mobile app and log into your verified account.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  3. Tap in the top right, then tap Meta Verified.
Instagram website (Desktop)
To view your Meta Verified subscription on Instagram:
  1. Log into your verified Instagram account on a computer.
  2. Click in the bottom left, then click Meta Verified.
