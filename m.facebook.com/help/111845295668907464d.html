Reply to comments on your Facebook Page's posts
Classic Pages on Facebook are updating to the new Pages experience. You’ll still have access to many of the same features to manage your professional presence, build your brand or business, grow your audience and connect with followers. Your content, insights, ads, Likes and followers will automatically transfer when your Page updates.
You can reply to comments on your Page's posts publicly or in a private message if you have Facebook access or task access to a new Page.
When you reply with a private message, anyone can see that your Page has responded privately below the comment.
New Pages Experience
To reply to comments as your Page, log into Facebook on a computer or the Facebook apps for Android or iOS.
