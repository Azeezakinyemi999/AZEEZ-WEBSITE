How do I change who can see the people, Pages and lists I follow on Facebook?
The people you follow can see that you're following them. To change who else can see the people, Pages and lists you're following:
  1. Scroll to the bottom of Facebook and tap Settings & Privacy.
  2. Scroll down to the Audience and Visibility section and tap Followers and Public Content.
  3. Tap Who can see the people, Pages and lists you follow?
  4. Tap the circle to select an audience.
Keep in mind that when you follow someone, a story about it may appear in your friends’ News Feed. People you’re subscribed to may receive a notification that you're following them, depending on their notification settings. They'll also see your name on their list of followers and may choose to make that list visible on their profile. If you choose to unfollow a friend, they won't get a notification that you've unfollowed them.
