Discrimination policies for listings on Facebook Marketplace
Commerce listings with discriminatory preferences go against our Commerce Policies and aren't allowed on Facebook.
Types of discriminatory preferences that aren't allowed include, but aren't limited to, these categories:
  • Race or color.
  • National origin.
  • Religion.
  • Sex or gender.
  • Presence of children.
  • Disability.
  • Marital status.
If your listing was removed for going against the Commerce Policies, learn what you can do.
Learn how to report a listing on Marketplace. Make sure that you select Discriminatory listing.
Examples of discriminatory preferences
Here are some examples of listings that go against our discrimination policies:
  • Description of a protected characteristic of the current occupants or neighborhood (example: “Christian family seeks roommate” or “Apartment available in a mostly Chinese neighborhood”).
  • Statements that certain people aren't allowed (example: “No seeing-eye dogs”).
  • Description of the housing that discourages people with protected characteristics from applying (example: “Units not good for wheelchairs”).
Note: If your listing is for a roommate or housing where you'll share common areas, it can include a preference for a sex or gender.
