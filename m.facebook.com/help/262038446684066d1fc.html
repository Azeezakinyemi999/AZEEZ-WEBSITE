Subscription for no ads
This subscription is only available in the European Region.
Due to changing laws, we’re introducing a new choice for how you see ads and use our Products. You can choose to subscribe to use our Products without ads or you can use them for free with ads. You are required to make this choice to continue to use our Products. This choice will apply to all your accounts in the same Accounts Center.
Here’s what to expect with your subscription:
  • When you subscribe, you won’t see ads on our Meta Products when you use your Facebook and Instagram accounts that are in the same Accounts Center. If you have any accounts that have not been added to this Accounts Center, you will also need to make a choice for those accounts to either use our Products for free with ads or subscribe for each separate account or Accounts Center. You can also add all your accounts to the same Accounts Center. Learn how to add or remove accounts in Accounts Center.
  • While you are subscribed, your information will not be used for ads.
  • Because you won’t see ads with your subscription, some ad settings will no longer be relevant to you, and you won’t have access to those settings.
  • You’ll still see posts and messages from businesses and creators, but you’ll no longer see their ads.
  • You’ll still have access to our usual features, like Feed, Stories and Reels.
  • Subscription payments are charged on a monthly basis, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before your next payment date through your Ad preferences.
  • Your ability to advertise and monetize with ads will be limited. Learn more about how your subscription may affect your ability to advertise or monetize with ads.
After you subscribe, you’ll stop seeing ads within 24 hours or after restarting your app.
Note: Facebook Lite users should close the app for at least 10 minutes to ensure you stop seeing ads once you subscribe.
Pricing of a subscription for no ads
The price you pay for a subscription for no ads on our Products depends on how many Facebook and Instagram accounts are in the Accounts Center where you have subscribed. It also depends on whether you subscribe through our websites or the Apple App Store or Google Play store, after taxes.
Note: You will only be charged for accounts on surfaces where we currently show ads, but your choice applies to all accounts added to the same Accounts Center. This means your information across those accounts will not be used for ads and you won’t see ads on any of our Meta Products when using those accounts. Additionally, prices may vary slightly from what’s listed below depending on the location you purchase your subscription due to currency exchange rate differences, local taxes and ordinances, and inconsistencies in the pricing tiers of in-app purchases using Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
Price
Subscribe on Facebook.com €9.99 per month for one account, plus €6 per month (or price in applicable local currency) for each additional account in the same Accounts Center
Subscribe in app using Apple App Store or Google Play Store €12.99 per month for one account, plus €8 per month (or price in applicable local currency) for each additional account in the same Accounts Center
If you don’t want to subscribe, you can choose to continue using our Products for free with ads. We are committed to your privacy and keeping your information secure whether you choose to use our Products for free with ads or subscribe. Learn more in the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Changing your subscription in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store
If you subscribed in app while signed into the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and want to cancel or make changes to your subscription for no ads, you must be signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you used when making the purchase. You won’t be able to cancel your subscription or add or remove accounts to your subscription unless you are signed into the correct Apple ID or Google account.
