Who can use Facebook Dating
Facebook Dating is available from the Facebook app for Android or iPhone.
Facebook Dating is available in many countries for adults with active Facebook accounts.
To access Facebook Dating, you must:
  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Have a Facebook account in good standing that is at least 30 days old.
  • Live in a country where Dating is available.
If you delete your Dating profile, you can't make a new one for 7 days.
