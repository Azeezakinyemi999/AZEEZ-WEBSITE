Report an app or game on Facebook
To give feedback or report an app or game:
  1. Scroll to the bottom of Facebook and tap Settings & Privacy.
  2. Tap Apps & Websites, then tap Logged in with Facebook.
  3. Tap Active, Expired or Removed, then tap the name of the app or game.
  4. Scroll down and tap Give Feedback about App.
  5. Select your issue, then tap Submit.
