Add or change your cover photo on Facebook
A cover photo is the larger photo at the top of your profile, above your profile picture. Like your profile picture, cover photos are public, which means anyone visiting your profile will be able to see them.
Add or change your cover photo
  1. Tap Profile at the top of Facebook.
  2. Tap Add Cover Photo.
  3. Tap Add Photo to upload a new photo, Choose From Recent Photos to choose a photo from your Facebook albums or Choose From Artwork.
  4. Tap the photo you'd like to use and then tap Confirm.
