Subscribe to Meta Verified for creators
This article is about Meta Verified for creators on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the Meta Verified for businesses Help Center.
Meta Verified for creators is a paid subscription that supports you across Instagram and Facebook. This subscription comes with benefits like the verified badge, account support, impersonation protection, and more.
Learn how to subscribe to Meta Verified below.

When you subscribe to Meta Verified, you’ll follow a verification process and submit an application for review.
You’ll choose the profile(s) you want to verify, set up your subscription payment, and confirm your identity.
You can verify up to two eligible profiles (one Instagram and one Facebook) that are added to the same Accounts Center. When you bundle your subscription and verify two profiles instead of one, you get a discounted price.

What to know before you subscribe

  • To qualify for Meta Verified, you need to meet the eligibility requirements.
  • If you subscribe two profiles, they need to be they need to be added to the same Accounts Center. Two-factor authentication also needs to be enabled on both profile(s).
  • If you don’t complete the verification process within 3 days, your subscription will be canceled and you’ll be eligible for a refund.
  • Once you start the verification process and submit your initial payment, you may not be able to change certain profile information. This includes username and date of birth on Instagram, and date of birth on Facebook.

Note: If you provide false or misleading information during the verification process, Meta may prevent your profile from being verified, remove your verified status, or take additional action on the account.

Subscribe one profile to Meta Verified

Instagram mobile app (iPhone)
Part 1: Select your profile and set up payment.
This is where you’ll choose the profile you want to verify and set up your subscription payment.

Note: If you have trouble with your payment, try these troubleshooting tips.
  1. Open the Instagram mobile app and log into your account.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  3. Tap in the top right, then tap Meta Verified.
  4. Select the profile you want to verify, then tap Single profile subscription.
  5. Tap Pay now, then confirm your payment through the Apple App Store.
Part 2: Turn on two-factor authentication.
This is where you’ll turn on two-factor authentication, a security feature that requires you to enter a login code each time you access your account on a device we don’t recognize.
  1. Select a security method on the screen, then tap Next.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions.
Part 3: Confirm or edit your profile information.
This is where you’ll choose to either confirm or edit your profile name and picture.

Note: After you’re verified, updating your profile name and picture may require you to go through the verification process again.
Select one of the options below.
Option 1: Confirm profile name and picture
  1. Review your profile name, then tap Confirm profile name.
  2. Review your profile picture, then tap Confirm profile picture.
Option 2: Edit profile name and picture
  1. Tap Edit profile name.
  2. Enter your first and last name on the screen, then tap Update profile name.
  3. Tap Upload a new picture.
  4. Tap Edit and select the picture you want to use, then tap Update profile picture.
  5. Review your profile edits, then tap Submit.
Part 4: Confirm your identity.
This is where you’ll upload photos of your government-issued photo ID (front and back) to confirm your identity.

Note: If you have trouble uploading your ID, try these troubleshooting tips.
  1. Select the type of ID you want to upload photos of, then tap Next.
  2. Tap Take photo, then take a photo of the front of your ID.
  3. Check that the information is readable, then tap Next.
  4. Take a photo of the back of your ID and make sure the information is readable, then tap Submit.
  5. Tap Finish to complete the verification process.

Once you complete the application process and submit your information, we’ll review it and send an update on your verification status within 3 business days.

Once you’re verified

After your application is reviewed and approved, your selected profile(s) will be verified and you’ll receive access to Meta Verified benefits, including support.
Note: Only Meta Verified subscribers can access Meta Verified Support. You’ll receive access to support after you’ve successfully completed the verification process.

Your verified badge will be displayed:
  • On your verified profile(s)
  • In the comments section next to your profile picture
  • In search results next to your name and profile picture
  • In direct messages next to your profile picture
  • In Reels, Stories, and Feed
