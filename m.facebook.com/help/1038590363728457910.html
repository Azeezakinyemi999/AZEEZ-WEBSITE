How do I adjust my mobile push notifications from Facebook?
When you've downloaded the Facebook app, we may send you two types of mobile notifications:
  • Push notifications: sent when you're not actively using Facebook (example: on your device's lockscreen).
  • In-app notifications: sent when you're using Facebook. These show up as numbers over the in Facebook's navigation, or over the Facebook app on your phone. You can't turn in-app notifications off completely, but you can choose what you get notified about.
Adjust your push notifications
  1. Tap in the top right of Facebook.
  2. Scroll down and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Notifications below Preferences.
  4. Scroll down and tap Push below Where You Receive Notifications.
  5. From here, tap to adjust how you get notifications and what you get notified about.
Turn off your push notifications
iOS
To adjust your mobile push notifications on your iPhone or iPad:
  1. Tap .
  2. Tap Notifications, then tap Facebook.
  3. Tap next to Allow Notifications to turn notifications from Facebook on or off.
Android
To adjust your mobile push notifications on your Android device (OS 6.0+):
  1. Go to your device settings.
  2. Tap Apps & notifications.
  3. Tap Facebook, then tap Notifications.
  4. Turn on or off notifications from Facebook.
