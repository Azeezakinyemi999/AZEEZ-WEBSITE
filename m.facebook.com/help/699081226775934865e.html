I'm having trouble downloading the latest beta version of the Facebook for Android app.
If you're having trouble downloading the latest beta version of Facebook for Android, try these troubleshooting steps:
  • Turn on automatic updates to get the latest beta version of Facebook for Android as soon as it's available.
  • Uninstall your Facebook app, restart your phone and then re-install the Facebook app from the Play Store.
  • Review Google's tips for troubleshooting download issues.
  • Make sure you're signed up as a beta tester.
