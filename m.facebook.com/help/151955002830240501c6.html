Reminders about voting and elections on Facebook
You may see reminders and posts about elections and voting. Facebook sends these reminders to help people make their voice heard during an election. These reminders are sent to people who are old enough to vote, no matter who they support.
Reminders
On Facebook, we use your age (18+) and location to show you reminders about election-related information, such as registration, vote-by-mail and Election Day reminders in your state or voting district. These reminders may include things like:
  • How to register to vote
  • Information about voting by mail
  • Notifications about local elections
To show you more specific voting reminders, we may ask you to provide additional information about where you live. Learn more about what information we use to determine your location.
Please note, information for voting and election reminders comes from state and local governments as well as our partners Ballotpedia and DemocracyWorks. These partners work with state and local governments to gather authoritative data about voting and elections.
Learn how to manage notifications about voting and elections.
Posts
During elections, you're also likely to see posts about voting from your friends. You can also use Facebook to share your own election experiences. The voting reminders that appear in Feed provide an opportunity to share.
What if I can't vote?
Information about elections may be shown to anyone on Facebook of voting age in countries where election reminders are available. We don’t try to determine who can vote, so you may still see election reminders even if you’re unable to vote. Countries and states have varying rules about eligibility to vote that can depend on age, nationality, criminal history and other factors. Learn more about voter eligibility on your local or national government website.
