Add or remove someone from your Restricted List on Facebook
Your Restricted list is for people you’ve added as a friend but may not want to share with (example: your boss). Learn more about friend lists on Facebook.
When you add someone to your Restricted list, you’ll still be friends with them on Facebook, but they'll only be able to see your public information (example: your posts and profile info you choose to make public) and posts you tag them in.
What will people on my Restricted list see when I share posts?
If you're friends with your boss, for example, and you add them to your Restricted list:
  • If you choose Public as the audience for your post, your boss will be able to see it.
  • If you choose Friends as the audience for your post, your boss won’t see it.
  • If you tag your boss in a post or photo, your boss will be able to see it.
If your boss goes to your profile, they won't be able to see anything you haven't tagged them in or shared as a public post.

To add someone to your Restricted list:
  1. Go to your friend's profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap See Friend Lists.
  3. Tap Restricted.
  4. Tap Done.

To view or edit your Restricted list:
  1. Log into Facebook on a computer.
  2. From your News Feed, click Friends in the left menu. If you don't see it, click See More.
  3. Click Custom Lists, then click Restricted.
  4. To remove someone from the list, click next to their name.
