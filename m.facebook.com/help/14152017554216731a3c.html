Log out of your mobile Facebook account if you don't have your phone
If you've lost your phone, you can log out of Facebook on a different device to prevent someone else from accessing your account.
Log out of Facebook from another device
  1. Log into Facebook on a computer.
  2. Go to your mobile settings.
  3. Click Activity log in the left menu.
  4. Scroll down and click Where you're logged in.
  5. Click next to the device you'd like to log out of.
  6. Click Log out.
