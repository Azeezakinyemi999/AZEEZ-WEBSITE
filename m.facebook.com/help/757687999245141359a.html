Earn a top contributor badge in a Facebook group
You can earn a top contributor badge in a Facebook group by being one of the most active participants in the group. The more you participate in a group and receive replies and reactions, the more points you’ll earn, and the more likely you’ll be to earn the top contributor badge. Members, admins and moderators can earn the badge.
When you earn a top contributor badge, it will appear by your name on your posts and comments and on your group member profile.
How to earn a top contributor badge
You can earn points and increase your chances of earning the top contributor badge in a group by:
  • Receiving replies and/or reactions on your posts
  • Receiving replies and/or reactions on your comments
  • Being an admin or moderator (If you want to become an admin or moderator, you can send a message to a current admin and ask them to invite you to become an admin or moderator.)
  • Being a group expert (If you want to become a group expert, you can send a message to a current admin and ask them to invite you to become a group expert.)
Troubleshooting
If you had a top contributor badge but no longer see it, it might be because:
  • Others in the group have become more active than you in the group.
  • The group admins turned off the Contributions feature.
If you know you’re the top contributor but haven’t earned the badge, it might be because:
  • The group admins turned off the Contributions feature.
  • It’s a public group and you aren’t a member or admin/moderator of the group.
More information
  • If you earn the top contributor badge but don’t want to display it, learn how to manage which badges appear by your name.
  • If you’re a group admin, you can turn the top contributor badge on or off by turning the Contributions feature on or off.
  • Learn more about group badges.
