Who can use Facebook Marketplace
Facebook Marketplace is available in many countries for adults with active Facebook accounts, and is available from the Facebook app for Android or iPhone.
You must be an adult, based on the laws in your country, in order to use Facebook Marketplace (example: at least 18 years old in the United States or at least 20 years old in Thailand).
To access Facebook Marketplace, you must:
  • Be an adult based on the laws in your country (example: at least 18 years old in the United States or at least 20 years old in Thailand).
  • Live in a country where Facebook Marketplace is available.
If you are an adult and incorrectly blocked from using Marketplace, you can verify your age to regain access. (Note: Some regions may have different verification methods available.)
Learn more about acceptable types of IDs for age verification or how to verify your age with a selfie.

We may restrict which Facebook accounts can access Marketplace. If you can't access or no longer have access to Marketplace, it may be because you:
  • Aren’t an adult based on the laws in your country (example: at least 18 years old in the United States or at least 20 years old in Thailand).
  • Have a new or inactive account.
  • Are using an additional Facebook profile instead of your main profile. Learn how to switch between your Facebook profiles.
  • Went against our Terms of Service, Commerce Policies or other policies.
