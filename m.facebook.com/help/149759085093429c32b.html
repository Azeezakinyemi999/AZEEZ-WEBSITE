Share photos or videos in a Facebook group
To upload an existing photo to your group, make sure the photo is saved to your camera roll.
Share photos or videos with a group
  1. Tap Groups at the top of Facebook and select your group. If you don't see Groups, tap Menu.
  2. Tap Photo at the top of your group and select your photo, then tap Done.
  3. After choosing your photo, write an optional caption and then tap Post.
In public groups, photos are visible to everyone in the audience of the group. In private groups, photos are only visible to other members.
Note: In public groups, admins might require approval before you can post or comment for the first time. The approval process might involve answering participation questions and agreeing to the group rules. Only admins and moderators can see your answers. Once someone is approved as a participant, their future posts might still require admin approval if the admin has set up post approval.
