Use Manage Activity in your Activity Log to archive or delete content
Right now, to manage your content with Manage Activity in your activity log, you must use the Android, iPhone or Facebook Lite app. Learn more about your activity log.
You can manage some of the content you share on Facebook, like posts, photos and videos, from the Manage Activity section of your activity log on the iPhone, Android or Facebook Lite app.
From here, you can select one or more pieces of content at a time and choose to:
  • Move to Archive. When you move content to your Archive, it’ll only be visible to you. If you choose to restore your content, it’ll be visible to the audience that was selected before it was moved to Archive.
  • Move to Trash. When you move content to your Trash, it’ll stay there until it is automatically deleted 30 days later. While it’s in your Trash, it’ll only be visible to you.
    • During the 30 days, you can restore your content to where you originally posted it on Facebook, move it to your Archive or you can select content to delete before the 30 days are over. When you restore your content, it’ll be visible to the audience that was selected before it was moved to Trash.
    • After 30 days, the content will be automatically deleted and can’t be restored.
Keep in mind, not all content in your activity log is able to be moved to Archive or Trash. When you choose Manage Activity in your activity log, you’ll only see the content that you’re able to move.

From your computer:
Right now, to manage your content with Manage Activity in your activity log, you’ll have to use the Android, iPhone or Facebook Lite app.
From the iPhone or Android app:
To archive your content
  1. Tap your profile picture in the top left to go to your profile.
  2. Tap below your profile picture, then tap Activity Log.
  3. Scroll down and tap the content you'd like to review (example: Tap Manage Your Posts to review things like photos, videos, text and status updates you've shared on Facebook).
  4. From here, you can tap Filters at the top to filter by things like:
    • Categories
    • Date
    • People
  5. When you find the content, tap on the right.
  6. If the content is able to be archived, tap Move to archive.
You can also move content that’s in Archive to Trash. In Archive, tap the box next to the content, then tap Trash at the bottom. Tap Move to Trash to confirm.
Keep in mind, once you've moved the content to Trash, it'll be deleted after 30 days and can't be restored.
To restore your archived content
After you’ve moved an item to your Archive, it’ll only be visible to you. When you restore your content, it’ll be visible to the same audience you chose before you archived your content.
  1. Tap your profile picture in the top left to go to your profile.
  2. Tap below your profile picture, then tap Activity Log.
  3. At the top of your activity log, tap Archive.
  4. From here:
    • If you see the content you want to restore: Tap the box next to the content, then tap Restore. Tap Restore to confirm.
    • If you don’t see the content you’d like to restore: Tap Filters at the top of your activity log, then filter your content by Date. Add a start and end date, then tap Done. When you find what you’d like to restore, tap the box next to the content, then tap Restore. Tap Restore to confirm.
To move your content to Trash
You can move content that’s in Manage Activity to your Trash. Keep in mind, when you move something to Trash it’ll be deleted after 30 days, unless you select content to be deleted before the 30 days are over. During the 30 days, you can restore your content. After 30 days, the content will be automatically deleted and can’t be restored.
  1. Tap your profile picture in the top left to go to your profile.
  2. Tap below your profile picture, then tap Activity Log.
  3. Scroll down and tap the content you'd like to review (example: Tap Manage Your Posts to review things like photos, videos, text and status updates you've shared on Facebook).
  4. From here, you can tap Filters at the top to filter by things like:
    • Categories
    • Date
    • People
  5. When you find the content, tap on the right.
  6. If the content is able to be deleted, tap Move to trash. Tap Move to trash to confirm.
You can also delete your content before the 30 days are over. Once you’re in your Trash, tap the box next to the content, then tap . Then tap Delete to confirm.
To restore content you’ve moved to Trash
After you’ve moved your content to Trash, it’ll be deleted after 30 days. During the 30 days, you can restore your content to where you originally posted it on Facebook. When you restore your content, it’ll be visible to the same audience you chose before you deleted your content. If you move your content to Archive, it’ll only be visible to you.
  1. Tap your profile picture in the top left to go to your profile.
  2. Tap below your profile picture, then tap Activity Log.
  3. At the top of your activity log, tap Trash.
  4. From here, you can:
    • Restore your content to where it was when you posted it: Tap the box next to the content, then tap Restore at the bottom. Tap Restore to confirm.
    • Move content to your Archive: Tap the box next to the content, then tap Archive at the bottom. Your content will move to your archive.

  1. At the top your activity log, tap Filters.

Other ways to archive your content
Keep in mind, your activity log only manages some of your content on Facebook. Other content you can archive includes:
  • Your Facebook Stories.
  • Messages on Facebook for the computer.
  • Messages on Facebook Messenger.
Keep in mind:
Content that you move to Trash is not deleted right away, but will be automatically deleted after 30 days. Unless you choose to delete the content earlier, during those 30 days the content remains subject to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
