About facebook.com on older versions of mobile browsers
In order to enhance security and optimize the user experience, we’ll be removing features from facebook.com on older versions of mobile browsers. Here are alternative ways to access Facebook:
  • Download the Facebook app for Android or iOS, or the Facebook Lite app if you have an Android device but a slow internet connection.
  • Visit facebook.com on a computer.
  • Visit facebook.com from one of these browsers, if it’s available to download on your mobile device:
    • Chrome (version 49 or higher)
    • Firefox (version 48 or higher)
    • Edge (any version)
    • Samsung (version 11 or higher)
    • Safari (version 12 or higher)
