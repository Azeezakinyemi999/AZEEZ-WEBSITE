Reactivate your Facebook account
If you’d like to come back to Facebook after you’ve deactivated your account, you can reactivate your account at any time. You can reactivate your main Facebook account or an additional profile.
Remember that you'll need to have access to the email or mobile number you use to log in. If you can't remember your password, you can request a new one. If you can't access the email associated with your account, learn how to reactivate your account.
