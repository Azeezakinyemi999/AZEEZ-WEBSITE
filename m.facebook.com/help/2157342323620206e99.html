Accept or remove a fan badge on Facebook
We are gradually enabling creators to set a custom top fan badge name for their most engaged fans. If a creator you engage with has set a custom top fan badge name and you are eligible to accept this badge by being one of their most engaged fans, you’ll receive a notification to accept the badge.
Any information in this article about the top fan badge also applies to a custom top fan badge name.
You can become eligible to display a rising fan or top fan badge for a creator’s Page or profile by being one of their most engaged fans.
  • Rising fans have earned a badge by starting to frequently engage with a creator by reacting to their content and commenting on or sharing their posts. They can be on their way to earning a top fan badge with continued engagement.
  • Top fans have earned a badge by continuously engaging with a creator by reacting to their content and commenting on or sharing their posts.
Accept a fan badge
Once notified of your rising fan or top fan status, select Accept badge from the notification to accept it. You can only qualify for one badge type at a time for every creator you earn a badge for.
If you accept a rising fan or top fan badge for a creator, or if you selected Turn on badges to earn badges for multiple creators at once, the badge(s) will appear next to your name when you comment on their posts. The creator may recognize rising fans or top fans in a public appreciation post that also displays your profile photo and name.
The badge is public, but it will only appear next to your name on the creator's Page or profile you're a rising fan or top fan of, not in your other interactions on Facebook.
Remove a fan badge
You can remove a top fan or rising fan badge at any time.
To stop displaying the badge, find a comment you’ve made on the creator’s Page or profile. Click the badge next to your name, select Manage Badge and toggle the badge on or off.
It may take some time for the changes to take effect.
How rising fan and top fan badges work
If you've accepted the rising fan or top fan badge:
  • The badge will appear next to your name in any comments you make on the creator's Page or profile you're a rising fan or top fan of, including your past comments. The badge will not appear in your other interactions on Facebook.
  • If you accept a rising fan or top fan badge, you automatically follow the Page or profile that the badge is for.
  • Rising fans and top fans are regularly updated, so it's possible to lose your fan badge, but you can earn it back if you become a top engager again.
  • Rising fans and top fans may gain access to exclusive perks. If you lose your rising fan or top fan badge, you'll no longer have access to these perks.
  • Creators can choose to remove individual rising fan or top fan badges, or turn off thes badges for their Page or profile. If this happens, you will lose your badge.
