Edit your post on Facebook
You can make changes to something you've shared on Facebook by editing your post.
Edit a post you've shared on Facebook
  1. Go to your post.
  2. Tap More below the post.
  3. Tap Edit, then tap Submit.
  4. Make your changes.
  5. Tap Save.
Note: You can only edit the text of a post. To add or remove photos or other items within the post, visit Facebook on a computer or use the Facebook app for Android and iPhone.
Note: If you're having trouble finding the post you want to edit, learn how to use your activity log to find it.
Learn more about how to change the audience of your post on Facebook. You can also learn how to delete a photo or video from your story on Facebook.
