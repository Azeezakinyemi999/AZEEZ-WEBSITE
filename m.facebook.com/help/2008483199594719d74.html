How do I delete a list on Facebook?
To delete a friend list you created, log into Facebook on a computer.
To delete a list you created:
  1. Log into Facebook on a computer.
  2. In the left menu of your Feed, click Friends. You may need to click See More first.
  3. Click Custom Lists and then click the list you want to delete.
  4. Click in the top right and select Delete List.
