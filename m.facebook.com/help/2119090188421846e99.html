Remove, ban or unban someone in a Facebook group you manage
Group admins and moderators can remove or ban someone from a group. Removed members will have to request to join the group again if they wish to rejoin. People who are banned won't be able to find the group in search or see any of the content within the group, and they can't be invited to the group again. They may still be able to see content previously sent in certain chats with Meta AI or other AIs while they were a member of the group.
Remove or ban someone
  1. Tap Groups at the top of Facebook and select your group. If you don't see Groups, tap Menu.
  2. Tap the name of the group.
  3. Tap Member at the top of the group.
  4. Tap View All Members.
  5. Tap Remove below the person you want to remove, then tap Confirm.
If you already banned someone and then later decide to prevent them from joining again under a different account, go to your group’s Members or People section, unban them, then ban them again and select Ban [name’s] future accounts. Note that Facebook may not be able to detect all new accounts.
Effect on additional profiles
If someone has additional profiles and you banned any of their profiles:
  • They can still participate in the group with any of their profiles that are already in the group.
  • They can't see, join or participate in the group with any of their profiles that are not already in the group.
If a group member has additional profiles and you removed any of their profiles, this has no effect on any of their other profiles, whether or not those profiles are already in the group.
Note that up to two profiles from the same account can join/participate in the same group. Learn more about additional profiles in groups.
