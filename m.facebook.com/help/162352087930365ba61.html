Why you can't access your Facebook group
If you're a group admin or moderator, you may have lost access to your group if:
  • Someone who helps manage your group changed your role.
  • Your account was hacked.
  • Your group was taken down. Learn how to appeal Facebook's decision to disable your group.
If you're a group member, you may have lost access to the group if:
  • The group was taken down.
  • You were removed or banned from the group by a group admin.
