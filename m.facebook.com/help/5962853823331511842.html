Eligibility requirements for Meta Verified creator subscriptions
This article is about Meta Verified for creators on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the Meta Verified for businesses Help Center.
Meta Verified for creators is a paid subscription that comes with features like the verified badge, account support, impersonation protection, and more.
Creators need to meet our eligibility requirements in order to qualify for Meta Verified.

Applicant eligibility

To subscribe to Meta Verified for creators, you need to meet the following requirements:
  • Be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your country.
  • Be located in a country where Meta Verified is available.
  • Have an eligible Instagram or Facebook account.
  • Have an eligible government-issued photo ID.
  • Agree to our Terms of Service and follow our Instagram Terms of Use, Facebook Terms of Service, Facebook Community Standards, and Instagram Community Guidelines.

Account eligibility

You can verify up to two eligible profiles (one Instagram and one Facebook) that are added to the same Accounts Center. When you verify two profiles, both profiles need to have two-factor authentication enabled and may need to have the same name and picture.
Note: Account eligibility requirements differ between Instagram and Facebook.

Instagram account eligibility
Your selected Instagram account needs to:
  • Be an Instagram personal or professional creator account (applicable in select countries) with a profile picture and a profile name that aligns with Meta Verified naming standards.
  • Not exceed the number of times you can make changes to your account within a certain time period (example: Instagram account type), applicable only in select countries.
  • Have two-factor authentication enabled on your profile.
  • Meet minimum activity requirements, such as prior posting history.
  • Follow our Instagram Terms of Use and Instagram Community Guidelines.

Facebook account eligibility
Your selected Facebook account needs to:
  • Be a personal Facebook account with a profile picture and a profile name that aligns with Facebook naming standards.
  • Have two-factor authentication enabled on your profile.
  • Meet minimum activity requirements, such as prior posting history.
  • Follow our Facebook Terms of Service and Facebook Community Standards.

Note: Eligibility requirements are subject to change as we improve the product experience for our customers.
