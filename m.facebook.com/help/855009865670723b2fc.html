How your shopping activity impacts data collection and the ads you see
As with other Meta Products, your shopping activity is used to personalize your experience and show you more relevant content, including ads.
Your activity on our Products may influence what you see in shops. Your activity in shops may influence what you see elsewhere on our Products.
If you make a purchase with Meta Pay, we’ll collect information in order to help complete your purchase, such as the payment method, transaction date, billing, shipping and contact details.
We will securely store and encrypt your payment card number. The payment card numbers you provide will not be used to personalize your experience or inform the ads you see without your consent.
To learn more about the information Meta collects and how it’s used, check out our Privacy Policy and About Facebook Ads.
