Leave the Facebook Lite Beta Program
You can only leave the Facebook Lite Beta program the same way you joined, either from the app or Google Play. You can leave the Beta program at any time.
Leave the Facebook Lite Beta program through the app
  1. Open the Facebook Lite app.
  2. From your Feed, tap in the top right.
  3. Scroll down to Settings & privacy and tap Early access.
  4. Toggle next to Beta program.
  5. Tap Leave to confirm.
Leave the Facebook Lite Beta program through Google Play
  1. Go to the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap LEAVE THE PROGRAM.
  3. Uninstall the test version of the Facebook Lite app from your Android mobile phone.
  4. Download the regular version of the Facebook Lite app from the Google Play Store.
