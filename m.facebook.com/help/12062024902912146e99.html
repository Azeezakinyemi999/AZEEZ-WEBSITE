About Meta Verified for creators
This article is about Meta Verified for creators on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the Meta Verified for businesses Help Center.
Meta Verified for creators is a paid subscription that comes with features like the verified badge, account support, impersonation protection, and more.

Per eligibility and where available, we offer several subscription benefits for Meta Verified creators.
Note: The benefits listed may not be available to all regions or creators.

Subscription benefits

Verified badge
  • A verified badge means an account has been verified based on their activity across our products or information and documents they provide.
Account impersonation protection
  • Help protect your account with proactive impersonation monitoring.
Meta Verified Support
  • Access email and chat support for help with account issues.
Enhanced profile
  • Add links with images to your creator profile.
Exclusive stickers
  • Get exclusive stickers on Stories and Reels.
100 Stars
  • Get 100 Stars per month to send to your favorite creators. (This benefit is only available for Meta Verified subscribers on Facebook.)

Getting verified

Creators need to meet our eligibility requirements in order to qualify for Meta Verified.
You’ll follow a verification process and submit an application for review.

Step 1. Choose your profile(s)
You’ll choose the profile(s) you want to verify and set up your subscription payment.
You can verify up to two eligible profiles (one Instagram and one Facebook) that are added to the same Accounts Center.
When you verify two profiles at the same time, you get a discounted price. Both profiles need to have two-factor authentication enabled and may need to have the same name and picture.

Learn about payments for Meta Verified creator subscriptions.

Step 2. Confirm your identity
You’ll confirm your identity and help secure your account with two-factor authentication.
Two-factor authentication is a security feature that helps protect your account by requiring a login code each time you access your account on a device we don’t recognize.
To confirm your identity, you’ll need to upload photos of a government-issued photo ID.

Step 3. Get reviewed
You’ll submit your information for review.
Once you complete the application process and submit your information, we’ll review it and update your verification status after our review is complete. This process can take up to 2 business days.
Learn how to subscribe to Meta Verified for creators.

Once you’re verified

After your application is reviewed and approved, you’ll receive access to Meta Verified benefits, including the verified badge.

Your verified badge will be displayed:
  • On your verified profile(s)
  • In the comments section next to your profile picture
  • In search results next to your name and profile picture
  • In direct messages next to your profile picture
  • In Reels, Stories, and Feed

Learn how to manage your Meta Verified creator subscription.

Existing subscribers

For existing Meta Verified subscribers
In certain regions, the Meta Verified creator subscription will now be called “Meta Verified Standard”. Existing subscribers will continue having access to the same benefits, unless we notify you otherwise.

For existing verified badge holders
If you already have an existing verified badge based on prior requirements, you can still subscribe to Meta Verified.

Important information

  • Meta Verified subscribers must agree to the Terms of Service and follow our Instagram Terms of Use, Facebook Terms of Service, Facebook Community Standards, and Instagram Community Guidelines.
  • If you provide false or misleading information during the verification process, Meta may prevent your profile from being verified, remove your verified status, or take additional action on the account.
  • After successfully completing verification, certain activity may result in Meta’s revocation of your subscription due to integrity concerns and potential fraud risk.
