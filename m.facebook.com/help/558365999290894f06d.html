How does Facebook protect my privacy when I buy Stars?
We value the trust you place in us. We take precautions to prevent unauthorized access to information you've saved on Facebook.
When you spend money on Facebook, we may share your contact or shipping info with sellers and their service providers according to our privacy policy. However, we don't share your payment card info or third-party payment login info with sellers, with the recipients of a donation, or any other merchant when you make a payment on Facebook.
Facebook may also store your info so you don't have to enter it again when you make a future purchase. If you don't want us to store your payment info for future use, you can remove the payment methods you've added to your account.
