Hide a comment from a post on your Facebook Page
Classic Pages on Facebook are updating to the new Pages experience. You’ll still have access to many of the same features to manage your professional presence, build your brand or business, grow your audience and connect with followers. Your content, insights, ads, Likes and followers will automatically transfer when your Page updates.
When you hide a comment from a post on your Page:
  • The comment will still be visible to the person who wrote it and their friends. This can help prevent more unwanted comments.
  • The comment will be hidden for everyone else.
  • Replies to the comment will also be hidden. If you want to hide specific replies to a comment, you can select and hide each reply separately. If you only hide replies to a comment, the original comment will still be visible to everyone.
Keep in mind: When you delete a comment from a post on your Page, the comment will be removed from the post. Learn how to delete comments from your Page.
New Pages Experience
The new Pages experience isn't available for all Pages yet. Learn more about the new Pages experience.
