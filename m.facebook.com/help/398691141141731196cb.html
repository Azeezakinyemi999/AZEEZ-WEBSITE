Share your posts from Facebook to Instagram automatically
There are some things to know before you set up automatic post sharing:
  • When sharing your Facebook posts or stories to Instagram, you can only share posts from your personal profile. If you have one or more Facebook business Pages, you can’t share any posts or stories you make on your Pages.
  • When sharing your Instagram posts to Facebook, if you have a personal Facebook profile and one or more Facebook business Pages, you can choose which profile or Page to share to from any of the Instagram accounts that you’ve added to Accounts Center. Your story and posts from those Instagram accounts will be shared to that Facebook profile or Page until you change it.
  • If you have a personal Facebook profile and one or more Facebook business Pages, you can choose which profile or Page to share to from any of the Instagram accounts that you’ve added to Accounts Center. Your story and posts from those Instagram accounts will be shared to that Facebook profile or Page until you change it.
  • Learn more about managing posting across your accounts in the Instagram Help Center:
    • Troubleshooting Instagram cross-posting issues with Accounts Center.
    • Why you’re seeing someone else’s Facebook information or posting to someone else’s Facebook account from Instagram.
    • How to share posts from your Instagram account to a Facebook Page that you manage.
    • What happens when you connect your Instagram account to a Facebook Page that you manage.
You can only set up automatic sharing from one Facebook account to one Instagram account. If you set up automatic sharing and then choose to share automatically to or from a different account, then the previous accounts will stop automatically sharing.
Set up automatic post sharing on the Android Facebook app
Note: You can only set up automatic sharing from one Facebook account to one Instagram account. If you set up automatic sharing and then choose to share automatically to or from a different account, then the previous accounts will stop automatically sharing.
  1. On your Android device, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the top right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Connected Experiences, then tap Sharing across profiles.
  6. Tap the Facebook account you want to share from.
  7. If you have multiple Instagram accounts in your Accounts Center, choose the account you want to share to.
  8. Tap next to Your Facebook posts to
    • Start sharing automatically.
    • Stop sharing automatically

Note: You can only set up automatic sharing from one Facebook account to one Instagram account. If you set up automatic sharing and then choose to share automatically to or from a different account, then the previous accounts will stop automatically sharing.
  1. On your iPhone, open the Facebook app.

Manage logging in with accounts
On your Android app
If you choose to turn off this connected experience, then you may be prompted to enter a password for your account. You may not be able to log into your accounts until you create a password. If you created an Instagram or Meta account with a Facebook account and you decide to remove that account from the same Accounts Center as your Facebook account, then you’ll be prompted to create a password for your Instagram or Meta account.
  1. On your Android, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the top right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. Tap Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Logging in with accounts.
  6. Tap:
    • Allow all accounts to log into each other
    • Choose which accounts can log into other accounts
      • To stop your Facebook account from logging into other accounts:
        1. Tap your Facebook account.
        2. Tap the checkbox next to the account you don’t want your Facebook account to access to unselect it.
      • To stop other accounts from logging into your Facebook account:
        1. Tap the account you don’t want to log into Facebook.
        2. Tap the checkbox next to your Facebook account to unselect it.

Sync your name, username, profile picture and/or avatar across profiles
There are a few things you should know before you sync your profile information across Facebook, Instagram and/or Meta Horizon:
  • To sync your profile picture, username or avatar, you also have to sync your name. This means that:
    • When you sync your name, your name will become the same across all of the profiles you’ve chosen to sync.
    • People will be able to see and search for you with this name and username on Facebook, Instagram and Meta Horizon.
    • People may also see suggestions (like to add you as a friend or to follow you) associated with this name, username, avatar and profile picture across Facebook, Instagram and Meta Horizon.
    • If you sync profile information across your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profiles, changes you make to your profile information (such as your name, username, profile picture and avatar) will appear across all of your profiles with synced info.
    • If you remove an account from an Accounts Center, your profile info for profiles in that account will no longer sync.
    • Note that you can’t sync profile information from a Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profile to another profile on the same product (for example, you can’t sync two Instagram profiles, or two Facebook profiles).
  • When you sync your profile picture, you can choose to use either your Facebook or Instagram profile picture across your accounts. The default picture will be the picture you most recently updated. To sync your profile pictures, you also have to sync your name.
  • In order to be able to sync a username, that username needs to be available on Facebook, Instagram, or Meta Horizon for the profiles you are trying to sync.
  • Changing your username will also change your Instagram account and Facebook timeline URLs.
  • Learn more about updating your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon username.
Sync name and profile on your Android app
  1. On your Android, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the top right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & Privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. Tap Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Profiles.
  6. Tap your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profile.
  7. Tap next to Sync Profile Info to turn name, username, profile picture and avatar syncing off or on.
  8. If you choose to turn syncing on, you can then choose to sync your name or your name and profile picture. To use a different profile picture, tap Change Profile Picture, then tap the circle next to the account that you’d like to use for your profile picture.
Sync name and profile on your iPhone app
  1. On your iPhone, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the bottom right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & Privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. Tap Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Profiles.
  6. Tap your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profile.
  7. Tap next to Sync Profile Info to turn name, username, profile picture and avatar syncing off or on.
  8. If you choose to turn syncing on, you can then choose to sync your name or your name and profile picture. To use a different profile picture, tap Change Profile Picture, then tap the circle next to the account that you’d like to use for your profile picture.
Sync your shopping activity across accounts
Sync shopping activity on your Android app
Note: Synced shopping activity isn’t available to everyone who has access to Accounts Center.
  1. On your Android, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the top right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. At the bottom, tap Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Shopping Activity.
  6. Choose the one Facebook account and one Instagram account that you want to sync shopping activity between.
  7. Tap to enable or disable syncing your shopping activity across Facebook and Instagram.
  8. If you have more than 2 accounts, tap the accounts you’d like to exclude from syncing.
Sync shopping activity on your iPhone app
Note: Synced shopping activity isn’t available to everyone who has access to Accounts Center.
  1. On your iPhone, open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap in the bottom right of Facebook.
  3. Scroll down and tap Settings & privacy, then tap Settings.
  4. Tap See more in Accounts Center.
  5. Tap Shopping Activity.
  6. Choose the one Facebook account and one Instagram account that you want to sync shopping activity between.
  7. Tap to enable or disable syncing your shopping activity across Facebook and Instagram.
  8. If you have more than 2 accounts, tap the accounts you’d like to exclude from syncing.
