Event Organised/Outreach

My Event

Organized the first ever National Engineering Workshop: Enabling 21st Century Skills for Engineering Development and Practices. Several software applications ranging from all Microsoft office suites, to other technical software’s such as MATLAB, C++, C#, Java, ANSYS Fluent software, Autodesk Product Design Suite, Solid works, MechDesigner, COMSOL Multiphysics and so on were taught using an online interactive platform.

Organized the first ever National Engineering Summit: Engineering and Social Economic Development in a Post-Covid Era. We hosted virtually NSE President, MD of TOTAL E&P, COREN President, APWEN President, Chevron Drilling Manager, NSE Vice President, Current Chairman of Nigerian Institute of Water Engineers and other Distinguished Engineers.

Application of Machine Learning in Engineering and Technology : Doing it the right way

Cutting Through the Hodgepodge : A Panacea to Post COVID-19 Era
