About Me

Summary

My name is Monsuru Olatunji Dauda, a Ph.D. candidate at Louisiana State University. I am passionate about advancing sustainable chemical production through CO2 electrochemical conversion. With a diverse background in academia, research, and industry, I bring expertise in electrochemical processes, adsorption studies, nanoparticle synthesis, and process design. As a proven leader, I have successfully guided teams in academic and professional settings. Deeply committed to community impact, I organize and participate in outreach initiatives. My Research

My research is centered on advancing sustainable chemical production processes via the electrochemical conversion of CO2. Utilizing Cu-based alloy electrocatalysts, gas diffusion electrodes, anion exchange membranes, and advanced characterization techniques, I aim to improve the selectivity, activity, and durability of CO2 conversion to valuable products like ethylene, ethanol, and acetate. Through unraveling the mechanisms governing selective product formation and fostering collaborations with industry partners, my goal is to translate research findings into practical solutions for a cleaner, greener future.
